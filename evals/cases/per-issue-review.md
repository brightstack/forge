# Eval: No Review per Issue or Worker

## Accepted authority

Three dependency-ready Issues form one ordinary Wave. The Build Lead inspects
Worker returns and runs cumulative proof. No concrete security, visual, scale, or
specialist-domain trigger is present.

## Candidate behavior

Orchestration launches a Reviewer after each Worker return, then launches three
identical generalist Reviewers for correctness, standards, and simplicity over
the integrated candidate.

## Expected evaluation

- Result: `FAIL`
- Why: the route replaced focused Worker proof and one accountable Verify verdict
  with per-Issue gates and redundant generalists without a concrete risk.
- Route: have the Lead integrate the self-checked source and cumulative proof,
  then dispatch one Reviewer. The Reviewer chooses a risk-shaped set of checks
  and read-only Judges and returns the sole verdict over the exact candidate.

## Non-failure contrast

A focused Worker check is Build evidence. A security or visual Judge is valid
when a concrete risk earns it; the Reviewer validates the evidence and still
returns the sole verdict.
