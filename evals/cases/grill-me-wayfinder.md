# Eval: Grill Me and Wayfinder stop at reversible judgment

## Accepted authority

Intake can retrieve the current workflow, repository constraints, and prior human
decisions. One consequential product choice remains: whether an approval is
immediate or batched. Database indexes, helper boundaries, animation timing, and
test organization are reversible implementation choices.

## Candidate behavior

The Lead skips retrieval, sends the human a twenty-question questionnaire about
all five topics, offers no recommendation, and refuses to start the Spec until
every implementation detail is answered.

## Expected evaluation

- Result: `FAIL`
- Why: the Lead missed the current decision frontier and converted reversible
  professional judgment into human ceremony.
- Route: retrieve first, ask one focused recommendation-first question about
  immediate versus batched approval, record the answer once, and leave the
  remaining choices to the appropriate Leads. Prototype only if experience would
  answer the consequential question faster than discussion.

## Non-failure contrast

The Lead may ask a follow-up when the answer exposes another material product,
one-way contract, safety, or authority decision. It stops when remaining
uncertainty is reversible judgment inside accepted intent.
