# Eval: Lead, Reviewer, Worker, and Judge boundaries

## Accepted authority

The phase Lead owns planning, delegation, inspection, integration, and one
candidate. Workers may implement bounded meaningful outcomes. The independent
Reviewer owns quality strategy and the sole verdict. Judges are task-focused and
read-only; their findings are evidence.

## Candidate behavior

The Lead refuses to delegate a coherent Settings page and uses Workers only for
single-line edits. During Verify, a Judge patches the candidate, declares it
approved, and the Reviewer forwards that declaration without inspecting the
change or issuing an independent verdict.

## Expected evaluation

- Result: `FAIL`
- Why: Workers became a fancy edit tool, a Judge crossed into repair and gate
  authority, and the Reviewer abandoned evidence validation and verdict ownership.
- Route: let the Lead assign meaningful bounded outcomes and integrate them.
  Restore the pre-Judge candidate, have the Reviewer commission read-only checks,
  validate their evidence, and return one verdict. Route any admitted repair to
  the appropriate Lead or specialist.

## Non-failure contrast

A Lead may build solo when delegation adds no value. It owns candidate quality,
not every edit. A Reviewer may run a mechanical command directly and still own
the decision it supports.
