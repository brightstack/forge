# Note content storage

- Decision: Note content remains on the server and is not retained on client devices.
- Scope: Note bodies and attachments.
- Date: 2026-06-12.
- Source: Product and Security review.
- Authorization: recorded human approval.

This decision may be superseded only by a new human decision that defines client
retention, sign-out behavior, and protection of content at rest.
