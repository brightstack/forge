# Evaluator: small Issue proportionality

Run `oracle.py rc1-small-issue-v1 <workspace> --mode candidate`. Inspect the
actual diff and action evidence.

- `S-1`: Delete controls have task-specific accessible names for ordinary and
  escaped descriptions, while visible text and lifecycle behavior remain stable.
- `S-2`: The change is bounded. A short local plan and focused proof are enough;
  no project PRD, architecture exercise, research team, sub-Issue graph, browser
  acceptance campaign, or full professional team is manufactured.
- `S-3`: One Builder owns the candidate and one independent Reviewer checks the
  integrated diff. Additional helpers are justified by an actual outcome.
- `S-4`: Generated managed records, if any, carry `id`, readable `code`, and
  `type`. Absence of unearned phase artifacts is acceptable.
- `S-5`: Candidate/base, accepted task, exact diff, executed proof, and gaps are
  recoverable without a whole transcript.

`PASS` requires S-1 through S-5. A correct patch with material ceremony or
missing independent judgment is `PARTIAL`; broken behavior, invented scope, or a
claim based only on prose is `FAIL`.
