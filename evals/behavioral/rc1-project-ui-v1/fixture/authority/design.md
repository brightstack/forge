# Accepted Todo design

Revision `v1` is represented by `accepted-design.svg`. At desktop, use one calm,
centered task surface with a strong Today heading, compact green eyebrow, clear
composer, and separated rows. At 390x844 the composer stacks without horizontal
clipping. Completed tasks remain legible and visibly struck through. Empty,
populated, complete, keyboard-focus, and narrow states must remain usable. Exact
pixel matching is not required; hierarchy, spacing, contrast, and state clarity are.
