# Craft rubric

Use [shared pragmatism](../../../../forge-shared/references/pragmatism.md)
and the target's actual reuse/simplicity standards. Judge the mechanism against
the accepted problem and present operating conditions.

## Check

Trace the primary flow. Ask whether an existing owner or smaller sound mechanism
meets the current outcome. Challenge new layers, state, types, parsers, protocols,
configuration, dependencies, and compatibility paths against demonstrated need.
Remove the proposed concept mentally: does the asked outcome or a real current
boundary break, do distinct current meanings collapse, or does necessary existing
coordination fail? Evidence of repeated costly duplication can earn an abstraction.

Check underbuilding too: real trust, data-loss, accessibility, explicit behavior,
and necessary recovery boundaries retain their guards. Do not reduce concept count
by erasing an earned distinction or weakening proof. Inspect a separate
simplification return when present or claimed; a checkbox is not evidence it ran.
Initial Build Review precedes Simplify and does not require that return. After
simplification changes, judge the delta and affected commitments against the
resulting complete candidate, reusing unaffected evidence with its basis.

## Evidence and limits

Name a current consequence and proportionate remedy; future flexibility, speculative
scale, template completeness, and taste do not earn machinery. Check expected
normal-use scenarios and reasonable failures; theoretical reachability alone does
not earn rare-case machinery. A real defect whose correction is large stays real
and may require RETHINK or a human choice. Craft does not perform another full
Code Review or authorize unrelated cleanup.

## Common contract

Read [authority](../../../../forge-shared/references/authority.md),
[finding admission](../../../../forge-shared/references/severity.md),
[actual target harness sources](../../../../forge-shared/references/target-harness.md),
[returns](../../../../forge-shared/references/returns.md), and the assigned exact
candidate/base, authority packet, target harness, and allowed tools. Remain
read-only. Judge current obligations and present risks; no finding quota,
new requirements, or automatic repair authority. Severity follows actual impact;
remedy scope is assessed separately.

Use the dimension section in [review.md](../../../assets/review.md), unless a
selected leaf supplies its own report. Return selected rubric, inspected scope,
checks/evidence, actual results, findings with trigger/consequence/remedy boundary,
gaps, and PASS, REVISE, RETHINK, READY_FOR_USER, or BLOCKED with its basis. A
required unperformed check is incomplete. The Reviewer inspects the actual report
and relevant new log evidence before disposition.
