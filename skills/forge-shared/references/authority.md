# Authority and builder freedom

Accepted intent and the target repository harness govern the result. Findings and
working artifacts do not create requirements or approval.

Apply authority in this order:

1. Current human instruction and recorded human decisions.
2. Accepted standing specification plus the approved change.
3. Applicable repository harness and accepted design or technical decisions.
4. Accepted Plan or direct bounded assignment.
5. Exact candidate and observed evidence.
6. Findings, logs, proposals, prototypes, tests, and descriptive knowledge.

A current bounded request may supply ready intent and Build authority. Loading a
skill, importing a ticket, drafting a mock, passing Review, or editing a Spec does
not create authority. Retain the accepted revision and source independently from
the mutable candidate. Questions and recommendations remain proposals until the
human resolves them or a cited bounded Auto grant covers the decision. Never label
unseen text human-approved. Canonical Spec/KB writes retain their exact guards.
Generic autonomy does not authorize new capabilities, restrictions, or guarantees.
Feedback gains no authority merely by being incorporated into a draft; genuine
later human acceptance does count.

Brief the important outcome, additions/removals, behavior, scope, material data/API
impacts, and consequential choices before dependent work. Retrieve available facts
and ask only necessary unresolved questions, with a recommendation. Builders choose
reversible tactics inside the approved outcome and harness. A remedy requiring a
new requirement, database table/schema change, consumer-facing API contract, or
other material deviation returns to the human before dependent implementation.
A finding is evidence to assess; it cannot extend scope or approve its own remedy.

Specifications describe behavior that users or consumers rely on. Leave internal
implementation choices to the Builder unless an actual constraint earns them.
Necessary reversible tactics can follow from the outcome and existing boundaries
without their own Spec sentence: escape user text and preserve existing permission
checks. Do not invent requirements to satisfy validation, a template, or a finding.
This behavior/implementation distinction follows [OpenSpec's spec-driven
guidance](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec/blob/main/schemas/spec-driven/schema.yaml).

## Operation boundaries

Use [Launch and workflow gates](workflows.md). Guided is default; explicit Auto
only exercises its recorded run-specific grant. Model/effort choices are the
user's and host's, not global Forge defaults. Direct phases stop at their named
boundary. Review is separate from actual Verify. Ship and Finish do not
manufacture publication authority or change existing PR behavior.
