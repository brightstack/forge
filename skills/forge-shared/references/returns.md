# Returns and owner inspection

Use the artifact template selected by the phase. This common contract supplements
it; it does not add a new report file for every assignment. Keep small returns in
the existing Issue, phase artifact, or labelled log section.

## Assignment return

- Outcome and requested stopping boundary; completed work or honest no-op.
- Exact artifact paths, candidate/base and dirty state when relevant, owned writes,
  and accepted authority/source revision.
- Applicable rubric/template and material checks actually performed.
- Evidence: command or inspection, source/path/session anchor, observed result,
  and the claim it supports. Distinguish inspected, executed, reused, and not run.
- Findings with shared severity, consequence, and remedy authority; integration
  concerns and material choices outside scope.
- Gaps, unsupported claims, remaining work, and relevant new log entries.

A compact evidence table is enough:

| Check or rubric obligation | Evidence/anchor | Actual result | Gap or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applicable check | Command, artifact section, or direct observation | Observed result | None, not run, stale, unavailable, or claim not established |

Use `NOT_APPLICABLE` with a reason when the check does not apply. Do not mark an
unperformed check complete or invent an observation to fill a template. An
instruction edit or structural link check is not behavioral evidence.

## Before advancing

The accountable owner opens the actual return, checks the selected template/rubric,
reads relevant new loop and Build-log entries since that assignment, and inspects
the cited evidence enough to assess its claim. Use the current assignment and
known prior state; no cursor ledger or polling service is needed. Check candidate
binding, scope, required independence, contradictory results, gaps, and whether a
finding is a supported defect or optional proposal, and whether its actual needed
correction exceeds authority. Persist delegated originals when required.

Record the owner disposition in the existing record: accepted handoff and its
basis, clarification/repair needed, or blocked boundary. Missing evidence stays
incomplete. Request only missing, stale, contradictory, or affected proof; do not
repeat every prior check. A return author cannot approve its own advancement.
Give concise reasons for rejected or optional received material feedback. Before
repair dispatch, apply the [defect and remedy check](repair.md); accepting a report
does not authorize all its recommendations.
